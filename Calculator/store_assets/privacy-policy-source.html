隱私權政策 - 小算盤 (Calculator)

最後更新日期：2026年8月

本隱私權政策說明「小算盤」App（以下稱「本 App」）在您使用時，如何處理相關資訊。

我們收集的資訊

本 App 本身不會要求註冊帳號，也不會收集您的姓名、電子郵件、電話等個人身分資訊。

  • 廣告識別碼（Advertising ID）：本 App 使用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告，AdMob 可能會收集裝置的廣告識別碼等資訊，用於投放與個人化廣告。這部分資料的處理方式請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。
  • 匯率換算功能：換算頁面會連線至第三方公開匯率資料服務（exchangerate-api.com）取得即時匯率，此請求不包含任何可識別您個人身分的資訊。
  • 裝置語言設定：App 會記住您選擇的顯示語言（中文/英文），僅儲存在您的裝置本機，不會上傳。

第三方服務

本 App 使用以下第三方服務：

  • Google AdMob（廣告服務）— 隱私權政策
  • exchangerate-api.com（匯率資料，公開資料服務，不涉及個人資料傳輸）

資料安全

本 App 不會將任何個人資料儲存於我們的伺服器，因為我們沒有伺服器 — 所有計算功能都在您的裝置本機執行。

兒童隱私

本 App 並非特別針對兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資訊。

政策變更

我們可能會不時更新本隱私權政策，更新後會修改本頁面的「最後更新日期」。

Privacy Policy - Calculator

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Calculator" app (the "App") handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

The App does not require account registration and does not collect personal identifying information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

  • Advertising ID: The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device advertising identifiers and related information for ad serving and personalization. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.
  • Currency conversion feature: The convert page fetches live exchange rates from a third-party public data service (exchangerate-api.com). This request does not include any personally identifiable information.
  • Language preference: The App remembers your chosen display language (Chinese/English) locally on your device only; it is never uploaded.

Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Privacy Policy
  • exchangerate-api.com (public exchange rate data, no personal data transmitted)

Data Security

The App does not store personal data on any server operated by us, because we do not operate any server — all calculations run locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected by the "Last updated" date on this page.
